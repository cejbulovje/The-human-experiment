PRIVACY

Before you share your answer publicly

You are free to imagine any outcome in the experiment. Before sending your main survey answer, leave out names, contact information and details that could identify real people.

What we collect

The main survey collects the answers you submit, your language, the submission date, a random browser participant ID and optional gender. Optional email is stored separately; see below.

Participant grouping

The browser stores a random participant ID locally and sends it with your answer. The ID can help prevent repeat submissions and link public answers. Clearing browser storage or changing browsers can create a new ID; it does not remove a public answer.

Bot prevention and technical data

Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical information to help distinguish people from automated traffic. The server uses a keyed hash derived from the connecting IP address for rate limiting; the raw IP address is not stored in the survey database by this application. Cloudflare may process network and device data under its own terms. Old rate-limit hashes are eligible for deletion after 24 hours. Cleanup runs when a submission reaches the rate-limit check.

Optional email for final feedback

If you choose to receive the final results and a thank-you message, your email address is stored in a separate table from public survey responses and is not included in the public results API. Providing an email is optional. The site currently collects this opt-in; sending the email will require the project to configure an email delivery service.

How responses are used

Public results and downloads can include written answers, selected choices, optional gender, language, submission date, and a participant ID where available. Older answers may also display a signature. Anyone can copy and analyse public data, and the site can use it for AI analysis.

Personal preferences preview

The personal preferences form keeps your answers in the open page. It does not send, store or publish them.
