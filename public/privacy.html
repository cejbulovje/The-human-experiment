How responses are handled

This is a draft privacy notice. Before public launch, replace the contact placeholder and verify it matches the actual deployment and applicable law.

What we collect

We collect the answers you submit, optional demographic responses, and the language version used. Do not include names, email addresses, contact details, or other identifying information in your written response.

Bot prevention and technical data

Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical information to help distinguish people from automated traffic. The server uses a keyed hash derived from the connecting IP address for rate limiting; the raw IP address is not stored in the survey database by this application, and old rate-limit hashes are deleted after 24 hours. Cloudflare may process network and device data under its own terms.

How responses are used

Responses are used to analyse the thought experiment. Public results are aggregated. Individual written answers are not published automatically and may only be shared after careful anonymisation and review.
