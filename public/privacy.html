PRIVACY

How responses are handled

What we collect

We collect the answers you submit, optional demographic responses, the language version used, and an optional display name or pseudonym if you choose to sign your answer. Do not include contact details or personal information about other people in your written response. If you want a signature, enter your own name or pseudonym in the separate signature field.

Participant grouping

To let people browse answers from the same participant, this browser stores a random participant ID in local storage and sends it with future submissions. Public answers submitted from this browser can be grouped together on the results page. This ID is not your name and does not verify your identity. Clearing browser storage or using another browser/device creates a different ID; older answers may remain unlinked.

Bot prevention and technical data

Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical information to help distinguish people from automated traffic. The server uses a keyed hash derived from the connecting IP address for rate limiting; the raw IP address is not stored in the survey database by this application, and old rate-limit hashes are deleted after 24 hours. Cloudflare may process network and device data under its own terms.

Optional email for final feedback

If you choose to receive the final results and a thank-you message, your email address is stored in a separate table from public survey responses and is not included in the public results API. Providing an email is optional. The site currently collects this opt-in; sending the email will require the project to configure an email delivery service.

Response deletion

After submitting, this browser receives a one-time deletion token. The token is stored in this browser so you can request deletion of your public response and its separately stored email opt-in. Only a hash of the token is stored on the server. Clearing browser storage or changing devices may remove your ability to use the token. Deleting a response resets the cumulative AI summary so it can be rebuilt from remaining public responses; it cannot retract copies or analyses already downloaded or made by others.

How responses are used

Responses are used to analyse the thought experiment. Written answers are published on the public results page alongside aggregate statistics. Answers are shown anonymously by default; if you choose to add a signature, that display name or pseudonym will appear with your answer. Display names are not verified. Do not include contact details or private information about other people.

AI-assisted analysis

The site’s AI feature processes public written answers in batches to create and update a cumulative summary, which is stored in the project database and used to answer questions about the experiment. The feature uses Cloudflare Workers AI (Meta Llama 3.2 1B Instruct). The summary and the last processed response ID are retained in the database with no fixed deletion schedule while this feature remains enabled. Public answers may therefore be processed by an AI service; do not include information you do not want published or analyzed.
