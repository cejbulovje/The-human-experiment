THE VOLUNTEER'S VOICE

What would people want for themselves?

These hypothetical preferences are a separate research category. They do not mean that a respondent has applied to become a volunteer.

Three separate kinds of participation

  1. Collective decisions: choices made by participants in the main experiment.
  2. Hypothetical preferences: what respondents say they would want if decisions were made about their own life. This prototype does not yet collect or publish these answers.
  3. Volunteers: people who separately and explicitly express interest in participation, then consent to the conditions of a specific scenario.

Comparative analysis

Once separate datasets exist, AI may compare collective choices with respondents' stated preferences and highlight recurring themes. Any analysis must show sample sizes and limitations; it should not be treated as a diagnosis or as proof of what all people want.

Explore the results with AI
