THE HUMAN EXPERIMENT

AN OPEN WORLDWIDE THOUGHT EXPERIMENT

“From today, you decide what happens to me.”

One person has voluntarily given humanity full power over their fate. Where they wake up tomorrow, what they look like in a year, who they become in ten years - anyone can have a say. Including you.

Imagine that anything people agree on can happen. You could change their body. Their personality. Even what they want. How far your own proposal goes is up to you.

What would you do with their life?

And if you were in their place - what decision about yourself would you entrust to strangers?

Decide another person’s fate

Imagine you could propose to humanity a decision about the volunteer’s life. How would you use that opportunity?

Propose a decision for someone else

Imagine being the volunteer

Imagine you voluntarily entrusted your own fate to humanity. What decisions would you want other people to make?
