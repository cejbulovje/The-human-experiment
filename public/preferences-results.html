AI INSIGHTS

Explore the results with AI

AI can help identify patterns in participants’ answers from around the world. For now, AI analysis is limited while the project is in its early stages.

As the project grows, we hope to expand the depth and capabilities of AI-powered analysis.

AI analysis is limited per connection and depends on the platform limits and computing resources available to the project.
